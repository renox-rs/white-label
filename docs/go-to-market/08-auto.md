# 08 — Auto Parts Suppliers & Fleet/Workshop Services: Go-to-Market Pack

> Status: draft · Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Source:

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Parts search, credit control, workshop job cards and insurance repairs for UAE
auto parts traders and workshops, in one branded system instead of WhatsApp, Excel and a
notebook.

**For:** auto parts traders in Deira, Sharjah Industrial Area, Al Quoz and Ras Al Khor that
sell to workshops, fleets and walk-ins, often with their own workshop; and independent
workshops that service fleets under contract or repair cars for insurers.

**Not for (say so early):**
- Traders who need licensed parts data (TecDoc) or VIN decoding from day one. Today the
  catalogue, cross-references and fitment come from the client's own data; licensed data and
  VIN decoding are on the roadmap.
- Agency dealerships already running a manufacturer's dealer system.
- Car rental, tyre storage (tyre hotel) and marketplace selling. Not covered.
- Total-loss and salvage handling. Not covered.
- One-person shops with a few hundred parts, who are better served by a basic POS.

## 4. Messages

### 4.1 Landing page

**Headline:** Find the part, sell it, get paid.

**Sub-headline:** Parts catalogue, credit control, workshop job cards and insurance repairs
for UAE auto parts traders and workshops, in one system with your brand, hosted in the UAE.

**Three problems we hear about:**
1. Orders arrive on WhatsApp with a photo of the old part, and only one person knows the
   cross-reference and the price.
2. Credit is given without limits; overdue workshops keep ordering and cheques are tracked
   in a notebook.
3. Insurance repairs stall between the surveyor, the approval and the excess, and insurer
   payments are chased by memory.

**Three outcomes:**
1. **The right part in seconds.** Search by part number, cross-reference across brands and
   grades, or by make, model and year; stock per branch; your price tiers.
2. **Credit that stops orders.** Credit limits per customer and group; orders over the limit
   or with overdue invoices go on hold until someone else releases them; cheques registered.
3. **Insurance jobs that close.** Claim, estimate, insurer approval per line,
   supplementaries, an invoice to the insurer and one to the customer for the excess, and a
   weekly statement of open claims to each insurer.

**Also included:** a B2B portal where workshops order and see their statement, deliveries
with proof and collected cheques, counter sales with VAT receipts and returns, reorder
suggestions, purchase orders in foreign currency with landed cost, workshop bookings and job
cards with estimates approved by link, fleet contracts with consolidated monthly invoices,
warranty claims to customers and suppliers, zero-rated export invoices with exit evidence
tracked, migration from your Excel files.

**Call to action:** See a 10-minute demo with a sample parts and workshop business.

**Footer facts:** hosted in the UAE · your own branded deployment · priced per branch /
workshop.

## 5. Demo storyline (10 minutes)

Uses *Deira Auto Spares & Service Centre* from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md) §08. Tell it
as the branch manager's day. Use fictional brands, insurers, VINs and plates only.

1. **08:00, the branch manager logs in** and sees what needs action: 4 workshops over
   their credit limit, a reorder list ready, an insurance repair waiting for the surveyor's
   approval, an order ready but not collected for 5 days.
2. **The counter:** a mechanic brings an old part. Counter staff search by the part number's
   cross-reference, or by make, model and year, see stock in each branch, and sell with a
   VAT receipt. *Wow 1: the right part across brands in seconds.*
3. **A B2B order:** a workshop orders in the portal; the order goes to the driver, who
   delivers with photo and signature and records a post-dated cheque.
4. **The workshop:** the service advisor books a car, checks it in with photos and
   mileage, sends the estimate for approval by link, and issues parts from stock to the job
   card.
5. **An insurance repair:** the insurer's approval is recorded per line; at the end, one
   invoice goes to the insurer for the approved amount and one to the owner for the excess,
   and the car is released once the excess is paid. *Wow 2: insurer and customer billed
   correctly from one job.*
6. **Close, the owner's dashboard:** margin by category, dead stock worth AED 180,000 and
   this morning's reorder suggestions. *Wow 3: cash tied up in parts that do not move.*

## 6. Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Our parts data is in Excel and in our heads." | That is the starting point: part numbers, cross-references, fitment and price lists are imported from your Excel files. Licensed parts data (TecDoc) and VIN decoding are on the roadmap, under a licence you hold. |
| "We use Tally." | Keep it for the books. We cover the catalogue, sales, credit, workshop and insurance work, and give your accountant a VAT summary; accounting export is on the roadmap. |
| "Can customers order on WhatsApp?" | WhatsApp is on the roadmap. Today workshops order in their own portal, and counter staff turn a WhatsApp photo into a search and an order. |
| "Insurers each have their own portal." | Yes, and no shared insurer–garage platform was found [S5]. Estimates go to the insurer as PDF by email, approvals are attached to the claim, and the insurer gets a weekly statement of open claims. An insurer portal and replacement cars are on the roadmap. |
| "We can't chase every cheque and limit." | Credit limits per customer and per group, automatic hold, release only by a second person, post-dated cheques registered, and receivables with statements and reminders. |
| "Can customers book and pay online?" | Online booking and online payment are on the roadmap. Today staff book slots, the customer approves the estimate by link, and payment is taken at collection or on account. |
| "We export to Africa." | Export invoices are zero-rated, with a packing list and a commercial invoice in USD; the exit certificate and bill of lading are tracked, with a warning before the 90-day limit [S6]. Customs declarations stay with your forwarder. |
| "We are a dealership on a manufacturer's system." | Then we are probably not for you. We fit independent traders and workshops. |
| "What does it cost?" | Priced per branch / workshop, with plans by team size. We quote after the demo. |
