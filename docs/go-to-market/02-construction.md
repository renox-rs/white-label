## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Attendance, materials, payment applications and project margin for UAE fit-out
and MEP subcontractors, in one branded system instead of Excel, paper and WhatsApp.

**For:** subcontractors in fit-out, MEP, joinery, facade and civil works that run several
sites at once for main contractors or directly for clients, and are paid through monthly
interim payment applications with retention and variations.

**Not for (say so early):**
- Companies that want project planning and Gantt charts. Clients keep MS Project or
  Primavera; we do not replace them.
- Companies whose main need is document exchange on Aconex or Procore. Not integrated;
  documents go to the main contractor as PDFs by email.
- Companies that want detailed cost build-up estimating. Rates are entered per BOQ line.
- Main contractors that mainly need to manage their own subcontractors' payment
  applications (payable side). Not covered today.
- Very small firms with one or two jobs a year, who are better served by a spreadsheet.

  Dubai Law 7/2025 every contractor, including in free zones, must be registered and must
  check that its own subcontractors are registered [S8].
- Several fit-out or MEP projects shown on its website or LinkedIn at the same time.
- Exhibits at or visits fit-out and building shows (§3).

## 4. Messages

### 4.1 Landing page

**Headline:** Know your margin before the project ends.

**Sub-headline:** Attendance, materials, payment applications and project cost for UAE
fit-out and MEP subcontractors, in one system with your brand, hosted in the UAE.

**Three problems we hear about:**
1. Labour hours and materials are not tied to projects, so the margin is known only when
   the job is finished.
2. The monthly payment application takes days in Excel, and retention, advance recovery and
   approved variations are easy to get wrong or forget.
3. Material requests arrive on WhatsApp and nobody sees what is approved, ordered or
   delivered to which site.

**Three outcomes:**
1. **Cost per project, as it happens.** Crew attendance marked on site from the phone, hours
   allocated to cost codes, and materials received or issued to the project; a warning when
   a cost code nears its budget.
2. **Payment applications without the spreadsheet.** A draft IPA at each cut-off; the QS enters
   progress, and approved variations, retention and advance recovery are calculated. The
   certified amount is recorded and the gap from the claim tracked.
3. **Materials under control.** Material requests against a cost code, approved by limits,
   turned into purchase orders and received at the site store; supplier bills matched to
   the PO and the delivery.

**Also included:** quotations from an imported BOQ, variations, retention release after
practical completion and the defects liability period, bank guarantees with expiry
reminders, daily site reports with photos, snag lists, worker records with visa and ID
expiry, timesheet export, a check on subcontractors' Dubai Municipality registration before
a PO on a Dubai project, VAT summary, migration from your Excel files.

**Call to action:** See a 10-minute demo with a sample fit-out company.

**Footer facts:** hosted in the UAE · your own branded deployment · priced per active project
or per worker on attendance.

## 5. Demo storyline (10 minutes)

Uses *Al Waha Interiors & Fit-out LLC* from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md) §02. Tell it as
the project manager's day.

1. **07:30, the project manager logs in** and sees what needs action: a cost code at 92% of
   its budget, 2 IPAs submitted more than 14 days ago without certification, a performance
   bond expiring in 25 days, a site with no daily report yesterday. Claimed vs certified vs
   received per project on the dashboard.
2. **The site supervisor's phone:** crew attendance in two taps, with the location recorded
   on site. *Wow 1: attendance on site, no paper.*
3. **The daily site report** arrives: manpower by trade, work done, photos and issues.
4. **A material request** above AED 50,000: the project manager approves, the MD approves
   as second step, and procurement turns it into a purchase order.
5. **Month end with the QS:** the draft IPA is already created; progress goes in per BOQ
   line, and approved variations, retention and advance recovery calculate themselves; the
   PDF is ready to submit. *Wow 2: the payment application that takes two days in Excel.*
6. **The certificate arrives:** the certified amount is recorded, the gap from the claim
   is shown, and the tax invoice is issued for the certified amount.
7. **Close:** the project cost report shows the over-spending cost code and the margin
   forecast. *Wow 3: they see the loss before the project ends.*

## 6. Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Excel works for us." | It works until the QS is on leave. The demo shows what Excel cannot do: warn you when a cost code nears its budget, remind you of uncertified IPAs and expiring bonds, and keep labour and materials tied to each project. Your BOQs and worker lists are the migration input. |
| "We already use Tally / Zoho / QuickBooks." | Keep it for the books. We cover projects, IPAs, attendance, materials and supplier bills, and give your accountant a VAT summary; accounting export is on the roadmap. |
| "We plan in Primavera / MS Project." | Keep it. We do not do planning or Gantt charts; we cover cost, cash and site records. If planning is your main problem, we are probably not for you. |
| "The main contractor wants everything on Aconex / Procore." | We do not integrate with them. IPAs, variations and reports are generated as PDFs you upload or email. If that exchange is the core of your work, we are probably not for you. |
| "Our sites have no signal." | The app needs a connection. Without one, the supervisor keeps a paper or photographed list and enters it within the late-entry window. An offline mode is on the roadmap. |
| "Does it produce the WPS salary file?" | Not yet. Approved timesheets are exported for your payroll today; payroll and the WPS file are on the roadmap. |
| "Can our clients see IPAs and variations online?" | Not yet; a client portal is on the roadmap. Today IPAs and variations go out as PDFs with photos. |
| "How long does it take to start?" | Your projects, BOQs, items and workers are imported from Excel; then the team is trained by role, starting with the supervisors. |
| "What does it cost?" | Priced per active project or per worker on attendance, with plans by size. We quote after the demo. |
