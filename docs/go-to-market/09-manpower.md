## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Visas, deployments, client-approved timesheets, invoices and WPS payroll for UAE
manpower agencies, in one branded system instead of Excel and email.

**For:** mid-sized agencies licensed by MoHRE for temporary employment and outsourcing that
employ and sponsor workers (cleaning, security, hospitality, labour, drivers, IT) and deploy
them to client sites, billing by hour, day or month.

**Not for (say so early):**
- Domestic worker offices (Tadbeer centres). A different licence and rule set; not covered.
- Pure recruitment or headhunting firms that place candidates but do not employ them. The
  candidate pipeline is on the roadmap, not in the product today.
- Agencies that need integration with government portals (MoHRE, ICP, GDRFA). Visa steps are
  recorded in the app, not submitted from it.
- Agencies whose clients require timesheets and invoices through their own vendor
  management system (e.g. SAP Fieldglass). Not covered.
- Agencies with a few dozen workers, who are better served by a spreadsheet and their bank's
  WPS template.

| Today | worker and visa dates in Excel, sign-in sheets or client biometrics, timesheets approved by email or paper, invoices and payroll in separate sheets, WPS file built by hand | already on a large HR suite that bills clients and is happy with it |

**Signals that a company fits**
- Job ads for a "payroll officer (WPS)", "PRO", "timesheet coordinator" or "billing
  executive" (manual work we remove).
- Several trades on its website (cleaning, security, hospitality, labour) and many client
  sites.
- Growth: a new large client contract, a new emirate, or a second legal entity.
- Exposure to the stricter WPS rules since 1 June 2026 (wages due by the 1st, new work
  permits suspended from day 5), which name recruitment, cleaning and security services
  among the targeted sectors [S5].

## 4. Messages

### 4.1 Landing page

**Headline:** From approved hours to invoice and WPS, without the spreadsheets.

**Sub-headline:** Visas, deployments, timesheets, invoices and payroll for UAE manpower
agencies, in one system with your brand, hosted in the UAE.

**Three problems we hear about:**
1. Visa, Emirates ID and insurance dates live in Excel; a missed renewal means fines and a
   worker who cannot be deployed.
2. Timesheets are approved by email or on paper, so invoices go out late and some hours are
   never billed.
3. Payroll and the WPS file are built by hand at month end, against a due date that no
   longer has a grace period.

**Three outcomes:**
1. **No surprise expiries.** Every worker document tracked by expiry, renewal cases opened
   before the visa ends, and deployments blocked when documents would expire on site.
2. **Clients approve, you bill.** Workers check in on site; clients approve timesheets in
   their portal or from an emailed link; invoices are drafted from the rate card with
   overtime and rest-day multipliers.
3. **Payroll from the same hours.** Payroll per legal entity with a second approver, the
   WPS salary file, payslips on the worker's phone, and alerts before the WPS due date.

**Also included:** client contracts and rate cards, manpower requests from clients,
replacements during leave, visa cases as tracked steps, leave with balances, final
settlements with gratuity, complaint and absence cases, receivables and cheques with bank
statement matching, margin per client and worker with visa and insurance costs spread
over the term, several legal entities with their own TRNs, migration from your Excel files.

**Call to action:** See a 10-minute demo with a sample agency.

**Footer facts:** hosted in the UAE · your own branded deployment · priced per deployed
worker.

## 5. Demo storyline (10 minutes)

Uses *Khaleej Workforce Solutions* from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md) §09. Tell it as the
account manager's week, with a look at payroll and the PRO's screen at the end. Use
fictional clients and workers only.

1. **Monday 08:30, the account manager logs in** and sees what needs action: a client
   approver who has not approved last week's timesheets, a deployment where no one checked
   in this morning, a payroll run waiting for second approval, 12 visas expiring within 60
   days.
2. **A client request:** the client asks for 10 cleaners in its portal. The account manager
   deploys them from the bench; one is blocked because their Emirates ID expires within 30
   days of the start date.
3. **On site:** workers check in on their phones inside the site's geofence; the site
   supervisor marks a worker without a phone.
4. **The client's portal:** the client approver sees only their own workers and approves
   the week's timesheets. *Wow 1: client approval without signed paper sheets.*
5. **The accountant:** the invoice is drafted from the rate card, with overtime and the
   Friday multiplier, and issued with VAT by the right legal entity.
6. **Payroll:** the payroll run per entity uses the same approved hours; a second user
   approves it and the WPS file is generated. *Wow 2: billing and payroll from the same
   approved hours.*
7. **Close, the PRO's dashboard:** visas and Emirates IDs by expiry, renewal cases already
   open. *Wow 3: renewals no longer missed.*

## 6. Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Our clients sign paper timesheets." | Fine: staff upload the signed sheet and mark the timesheet approved with the file attached. Clients who prefer it approve in the portal or from an emailed link, without a full login. |
| "Our workers have no email." | Workers sign in to the worker app with their mobile number and an SMS code. A supervisor checks in workers without a phone. WhatsApp is on the roadmap. |
| "Does it submit visas to MoHRE or GDRFA?" | No. No public government API for agencies was found [S6]. Each visa case tracks its steps, documents, dates and fees, and renewal cases open automatically before expiry. |
| "Does it pay salaries?" | It generates the WPS salary file per legal entity for your bank or exchange house to upload, and publishes payslips once payment is confirmed. It flags a payroll that is not paid before the due date and on days 2 and 5. |
| "We use Tally / Zoho for accounts." | Keep it for the books. We cover workers, deployments, billing, payroll and receivables, and give your accountant a VAT summary per entity; accounting export is on the roadmap. |
| "We also recruit candidates." | The candidate pipeline is on the roadmap. Today workers enter as employees with a visa case. |
| "We need rosters, accommodation and Emiratisation tracking." | Shift rostering, accommodation and transport, and Emiratisation tracking are on the roadmap. |
| "We supply domestic workers." | Not covered; Tadbeer is a different licence and rule set. Probably not for you. |
| "What does it cost?" | Priced per deployed worker, with plans by team size. We quote after the demo. |
