# 10 — Training Centres & EdTech: Go-to-Market Pack

> Status: draft · Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Source:

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Enquiries, trials, timetables, attendance, fees and certificates for UAE
training and tutoring centres, with a parent portal and WhatsApp updates, instead of
WhatsApp threads and spreadsheets.

**For:** licensed training institutes and tutoring centres (KHDA in Dubai, ACTVET in Abu
Dhabi, SPEA in Sharjah) that sell courses, session packages and monthly memberships for
professional certification, languages, IT skills, test preparation and school tutoring,
often to minors through their parents.

**Not for (say so early):**
- Centres that want a full LMS: SCORM, auto-scored quizzes, video hosting.
- Online-only course businesses that need video meetings created automatically (class links
  are pasted per session for now).
- Corporate-training-only firms that need quotations and a corporate client portal (on the
  roadmap).
- Schools and universities, and individual private tutors working alone.
- Centres that need payroll for employed tutors, or billing in several currencies.

## 2. Ideal client profile

| | Fits | Weak fit |
|---|---|---|
| Students | 150–3,000 active students, 20–200 classes a week | under 50 students |
| Team | 5–60 tutors (many part-time or freelance), counsellors, coordinator, accountant | one tutor-owner |
| Location | Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah; 1–5 branches | — |
| Model | courses, session packages and memberships, often mixed; some corporate cohorts | corporate cohorts only |
| Students | many minors, enrolled and paid for by a parent | — |
| Today | WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries, timetable images, paper or Google Form attendance, fees in a notebook or the accounting tool, certificates in Word | already on a school or training management system and happy with it |

**Signals that a company fits**
- Listed in the KHDA training institutes directory or the SPEA training centres directory.
- Instagram posts selling packages ("10 sessions"), trial classes or sibling discounts.
- Job ads for an "admissions counsellor", "front desk coordinator" or "academic
  coordinator".
- A second branch or a new course category (e.g. test preparation, coding for kids).

**Who decides and who uses it**

| Role | Cares about | Message angle |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Revenue per course, unpaid fees, growth | Which courses make money; fees collected on time |
| Centre manager | Batch fill rates, tutor cover, parent complaints | Batches, attendance and fees on one screen |
| Admissions counsellor | Leads lost on WhatsApp, trial conversion | Every enquiry followed up, trial to enrolment in one flow |
| Accountant | Instalments, failed card payments, refunds, tutor bills | Memberships charged and retried, refunds per policy, freelance tutor bills from sessions taught |

## 4. Messages

### 4.1 Landing page

**Headline:** From WhatsApp enquiry to certificate, in one place.

**Sub-headline:** Admissions, timetables, attendance, fees and certificates for UAE training
centres, with a branded portal for students and parents, hosted in the UAE.

**Three problems we hear about:**
1. Enquiries arrive on WhatsApp and Instagram. Some get a trial class, some get forgotten.
2. Attendance is on paper, so nobody knows how many sessions are left in a package until a
   parent asks.
3. Fees are chased by hand: instalments, failed card payments and refunds in a notebook.

**Three outcomes:**
1. **Every enquiry followed up.** Leads, trial classes and placement tests, then enrolment
   with discounts and e-signed terms and guardian consent.
2. **Parents see it the same day.** Tutors mark attendance on a phone, package credits are
   used, and parents see attendance and results in the portal, with WhatsApp updates.
3. **Fees that collect themselves.** Instalments and monthly memberships charged by card,
   failed payments retried, online payment links, refunds calculated from your policy.

**Also included:** courses with regulator approvals recorded, batches with tutor and room
allocation, sessions generated around public holidays, make-ups and transfers, materials per
course, assessments and gradebook, certificates in English and Arabic with a QR code and
public verification page, freelance tutor bills from sessions taught, tutor permit expiry
reminders, dashboards, migration from Excel.

**Call to action:** See a 10-minute demo with a sample centre.

**Footer facts:** hosted in the UAE · your own branded deployment · priced per active
student.

## 5. Demo storyline (10 minutes)

Uses *Noor Learning Centre* from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md) §10. Tell it as the
centre manager's day. All students are fictional; no minors' photos.

1. **09:00, the centre manager opens the dashboard:** 3 leads with no follow-up for a week,
   a batch below minimum size, a tutor permit expiring, failed membership payments in retry,
   packages expiring with credits left.
2. **Admissions:** a parent's WhatsApp enquiry is recorded as a lead; the counsellor books a
   trial class from the desk and a reminder goes out. After the trial the tutor enters the
   level and the system suggests a batch.
3. **Enrolment:** the child is enrolled with a sibling discount; terms, refund policy and
   guardian consent are signed electronically; the first payment is taken by an online
   link.
4. **The tutor's phone:** attendance marked for the session; a package credit is used.
   *Wow 1: the parent sees attendance in the portal the same day.*
5. **The accountant:** a monthly membership charged by card; a failed payment retried and
   the guardian notified.
6. **Certificate:** an adult student completes a course; a certificate is issued in English
   and Arabic and verified from its QR code on the public page. *Wow 2: verifiable
   certificates.*
7. **Back to the manager:** batch fill rates, lead conversion and revenue per course.
   *Wow 3: which courses actually make money.*

## 6. Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "WhatsApp and Google Sheets work for us." | They work until the person who knows the sheet is away. The demo shows what they cannot do: use a package credit when attendance is marked, retry a failed card payment, and issue a certificate anyone can verify. Your sheets are the migration input. |
| "Can parents book trials online?" | On the roadmap. Today the counsellor books trials from the desk in seconds, and reminders go out automatically. |
| "We need a full LMS with quizzes and videos." | Then we are probably not for you. We include materials, assessments and a gradebook; assignment submissions and auto-scored quizzes are on the roadmap; SCORM and video hosting are not planned. |
| "We teach online too." | You paste the class link per session. Creating Zoom, Teams or Meet links automatically is on the roadmap. |
| "Will the certificate say KHDA approved?" | Only if you record the approval or attestation for that course. The system never prints an approval the course does not have. |
| "Parents want progress reports." | Attendance and published assessment results are in the parent portal today; scheduled progress reports are on the roadmap. |
| "We train company staff." | You can enrol the cohort and invoice the company. Quotations and a corporate client portal are on the roadmap. |
| "Most of our students are children. Is their data safe?" | Each centre has its own deployment, hosted in the UAE. Guardian consent is recorded for data and photos; tutors do not see minors' contact details; every action is logged. |
| "What does it cost?" | Priced per active student, with plans by centre size. We quote after the demo. |
