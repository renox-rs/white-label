## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Deadlines, document requests, AML files and retainer billing for UAE accounting
and tax firms, in one branded system with a client portal instead of shared drives,
WhatsApp and an Excel tracker.

**For:** accounting, tax and fractional-CFO firms in the UAE that serve SME clients on
monthly retainers (bookkeeping, VAT, corporate tax, CFO services), usually a registered tax
agency, and that must keep CDD and sanctions screening files as a DNFBP.

**Not for (say so early):**
- Firms looking for a general ledger or bookkeeping software. Clients' books stay in Zoho
  Books, Xero, QuickBooks or Tally.
- Firms that want returns filed straight to EmaraTax or reports sent straight to goAML from
  the system. Filing stays manual; the system records the evidence.
- Audit firms looking for an audit methodology (working papers per auditing standards).
- Payroll processing for clients, and ESR work.
- Sole practitioners with a few dozen clients, who are better served by a spreadsheet.

## 4. Messages

### 4.1 Landing page

**Headline:** Every client's deadlines, documents and AML files in one place.

**Sub-headline:** A practice system for UAE accounting and tax firms, with a client portal
under your brand, hosted in the UAE.

**Three problems we hear about:**
1. Each client has its own VAT stagger, financial year and corporate tax dates, tracked in
   one Excel file that one person understands.
2. Documents arrive on WhatsApp and email, half complete, a few days before the return is
   due.
3. CDD files and screening results sit in scanned folders, and nobody knows which reviews
   are due.

**Three outcomes:**
1. **A calendar that builds itself.** Deadlines generated from each client's tax profile,
   each one a task with an owner, reminders and the FTA acknowledgement attached at the end.
2. **Clients upload against a list.** A branded portal with each period's document request
   list; clients upload from a phone, and late items are reminded automatically.
3. **AML files in order.** Beneficial owners, sanctions screening against the UN and UAE
   lists, risk rating and approval, scheduled CDD reviews, and an AML case log only the MLRO
   sees.

**Also included:** VAT and corporate tax return workflows with a separate reviewer and
client approval in the portal, engagement letters accepted in the portal, time entries,
retainers and WIP billing, invoices, payments and cheques, month-end close checklists, KPI
packs from imported trial balances (Zoho Books, Xero, QuickBooks, Tally), client groups with
one login for a group CFO, firm dashboards, migration from your Excel files.

**Call to action:** See a 10-minute demo with a sample firm.

**Footer facts:** hosted in the UAE · your own branded deployment · priced per client
company (engagement).

## 5. Demo storyline (10 minutes)

Uses *Bayan Accounting & Tax Consultants* from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md) §04. Tell it
as the manager's day before a VAT deadline, with the MLRO and the partner joining.

1. **08:30, the manager opens the deadline calendar:** this month's VAT returns by status;
   6 are due in 5 days with documents still missing.
2. **One client's request list:** what is outstanding, what was rejected with a reason, and
   the reminders already sent.
3. **The client's phone:** the finance contact sees the request list in the portal and
   uploads the purchase invoices; the manager accepts them. *Wow 1: no more chasing
   documents on WhatsApp.*
4. **A return:** prepared by an accountant, reviewed by the manager (the preparer cannot
   review it), approved by the client approver in the portal. After filing on EmaraTax the
   acknowledgement is attached and the task closes.
5. **The MLRO:** a high-risk client's EDD file, 3 CDD reviews due, and a re-screening hit
   waiting for a decision (fictional list). *Wow 2: AML evidence in one place for an
   inspection.*
6. **The partner dashboard:** WIP, realisation and retainers by client; 8 retainers past
   due; one engagement with low realisation. *Wow 3: which clients lose money.*
7. **Close:** a client's KPI pack, built from an imported trial balance and published to the
   portal.

## 6. Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Our Excel tracker works." | It works until a manager is on leave in a VAT peak. The demo shows what Excel cannot do: generate each client's dates, remind the client, and close a deadline only with the acknowledgement attached. Your tracker is the migration input. |
| "Does it file with the FTA or goAML?" | No. You file on EmaraTax and goAML as today; the system records who filed, the acknowledgement or goAML reference, and locks the filed return. |
| "Will it replace Zoho / Xero for our clients' books?" | No, it is not a ledger. Books stay where they are; you import the trial balance for KPI packs. Live connectors to Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks are on the roadmap. |
| "Our clients won't use a portal." | They only need the request list and an upload button, on a phone, with email reminders. WhatsApp reminders are on the roadmap. |
| "Is the screening good enough for MoET?" | It screens your client parties against the UN Consolidated List and the UAE Local Terrorist List, re-screens when lists change, and alerts the MLRO at once. The MLRO decides and reports. Commercial screening providers are on the roadmap. We do not certify your compliance. |
| "Client data is confidential." | Each firm has its own deployment, hosted in the UAE. Staff see only the clients they are assigned to; every view of passports and bank statements is logged; staff log in with two-factor authentication. |
| "Is portal acceptance a real signature?" | The client accepts after signing in; name, time, IP address and document hash are recorded in an acceptance certificate. UAE PASS signing is on the roadmap for letters that need more legal weight. |
| "We already run a full practice management suite." | Then we are probably not for you, unless it lacks UAE tax calendars and AML files. |
| "What does it cost?" | Priced per client company (engagement), with plans by firm size. We quote after the demo. |
