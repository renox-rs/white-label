# Go-to-Market Packs

> Status: draft · Last updated: 2026-10-15

The ten vertical templates are how we find clients: each one gives a specific kind of UAE SME
a reason to talk to us. These packs turn the accepted vertical documents into material for
finding and approaching leads. They are written for us and for whoever helps with sales,
not for the client.

One pack per vertical, all with the same sections:

| § | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Positioning | One line, who it is for, who it is not for |
| 2 | Ideal client profile | Size, location, signals that a company fits, who decides |
| 3 | Lead sources | Where such companies can be found, with terms of use |
| 4 | Messages | Landing page copy, first email, LinkedIn note, follow-up |
| 5 | Demo storyline | The 10-minute demo from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md), told as the client's day |
| 6 | Objections | Common objections and honest answers |
| 7 | What we can claim | MVP features we may promise; Later features we only call "on the roadmap" |
| — | Sources | As in the vertical documents |

## Rules for every pack

1. **Claim only the MVP.** Every promise points to an MVP capability in the vertical's §12.
   Later items are named "on the roadmap", never as available. Out-of-scope items are not
   mentioned as coming.
2. **No prices until [PRICING.md](../PRICING.md) is filled.** Copy says "priced per managed
   unit" (or the vertical's metric), not a number.
3. **Lead lists are built by hand.** Official registries and portals are looked up one
   company at a time; we do not scrape or bulk-copy them, because their terms limit use to
   personal or non-commercial purposes (see each pack's §3).
4. **Outreach follows UAE rules** (until a lawyer answers [OPEN-ITEMS.md](../OPEN-ITEMS.md)
   L-18):
   - **Email:** to a company's published business address or to a person's business
     address, short and relevant to their role, with our identity and a one-click opt-out;
     we record where each contact came from and honour objections at once (PDPL Art. 17).
   - **Calls:** none until we hold TDRA telemarketing approval; then only from a licensed
     local number, 09:00–18:00, never to numbers on the Do Not Call register, and never
     again after a refusal (Cabinet Resolution 56 of 2024).
   - **SMS and WhatsApp:** only after the person has agreed to hear from us (TDRA
     opt-in rules). Never as a first contact.
   - **LinkedIn:** manual connection requests and messages; no automation tools.
5. **Fictional demo data only.** Demos use the companies in [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md),
   never a prospect's real data before a contract.
6. **English first.** Arabic versions of landing copy come with the Arabic UI.
