## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Leases, cheques, maintenance and owner statements for UAE property managers,
in one branded system instead of Excel and WhatsApp.

**For:** property management companies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi that manage residential and
commercial units for landlords, and landlords or family offices that manage their own
portfolio with an in-house team.

**Not for (say so early):**
- Owners' association and service charge management (Mollak). Out of scope; companies that do
  both are approached for their leasing and rental management work only.
- Brokerage only (sales listings, commissions). Listing portals are not integrated.
- Holiday homes and short stays (nightly bookings, channel managers).
- Single landlords with a handful of units, who are better served by a spreadsheet.

## 2. Ideal client profile

| | Fits | Weak fit |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio | 200–5,000 units, one or several buildings | under 100 units |
| Team | 5–50 staff: property managers, leasing, accounts, maintenance coordinator | one person does everything |
| Location | Dubai, Abu Dhabi (Sharjah and others once AU-08 and AU-09 are answered) | — |
| Model | manages for many owners (fees, owner statements), or owns a portfolio | brokerage only |
| Payment practice | rent mostly by post-dated cheques, with transfers growing | — |
| Today | Excel rent roll and cheque register, WhatsApp requests, Zoho/QuickBooks/Tally for the books | already on Yardi, MRI or a large ERP and happy with it |

**Signals that a company fits**
- Job ads for a "leasing administrator", "cheque/collections officer" or "property
  accountant" (manual work we remove).
- Several buildings in its name on Bayut or Property Finder, or tenants complaining online
  about slow maintenance or deposit refunds.
- A new building handed over to it, or a new owner portfolio added.
- Registered as a real estate company managing property for third parties.

**Who decides and who uses it**

| Role | Cares about | Message angle |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / managing director | Owners kept happy, staff cost, arrears | Owner statements and arrears without a month-end scramble |
| Head of property management | Renewals, occupancy, tenant complaints | Nothing expires unnoticed; tenants raise requests themselves |
| Finance manager / accountant | Cheques, bounced cheques, bank matching, VAT | Today's cheque list, bounced cheques followed up, VAT summary per entity |
| Maintenance coordinator | Tickets, vendors, AMC visits and certificates | Every request with photos and SLA; AMC visits and certificates tracked |

## 4. Messages

### 4.1 Landing page

**Headline:** Run your rentals without the spreadsheet.

**Sub-headline:** Leases, cheques, maintenance and owner statements for UAE property managers,
in one system with your brand, hosted in the UAE.

**Three problems we hear about:**
1. Cheques live in a spreadsheet. Someone remembers which to deposit, and bounced cheques
   are chased by memory.
2. Tenants send maintenance requests on WhatsApp. Nobody sees what is open, late or done.
3. Month end means building each owner's statement by hand from bank exports and invoices.

**Three outcomes:**
1. **Every cheque on time.** Today's deposit list, bounced cheques flagged with a follow-up
   task, and bank transfers matched to open invoices from your statement file.
2. **Tenants help themselves.** A branded tenant portal to see invoices and cheques, raise a
   request with photos and follow it; technicians and vendors update it from their phone.
3. **Owner statements in minutes.** Rent, expenses, AMC costs and your management fee per
   owner, as a PDF ready to send.

**Also included:** bilingual (English/Arabic) tenancy contracts, renewal and move-out flows
with approved deposit deductions, one tax invoice per instalment with the right VAT per line,
several legal entities with their own TRNs, AMC contracts with planned visits and fire and
lift certificates, VAT summary per entity, migration from your Excel files.

**Call to action:** See a 10-minute demo with a sample portfolio.

**Footer facts:** hosted in the UAE · your own branded deployment · priced per managed unit.

## 5. Demo storyline (10 minutes)

Uses *Sahraa Property Management LLC* from [DEMO-DATA.md](../DEMO-DATA.md) §01. Tell it as
the property manager's morning.

1. **08:30, the property manager logs in** and sees what needs action: 9 leases in the
   renewal window, 2 tickets past SLA, 1 lease without an Ejari number. Occupancy and
   collections on the dashboard.
2. **One page per lease:** tenant, unit, documents, cheques and invoices together.
3. **A renewal:** new dates, new cheques, the contract PDF in English and Arabic. *Wow 1.*
4. **The tenant's phone:** the tenant portal shows invoices and the cheque schedule; the
   tenant raises an AC request with a photo.
5. **Back office:** the request appears, goes to the AC vendor, and the vendor closes it from
   the phone with before and after photos; the tenant is told. *Wow 2.*
6. **The accountant:** today's cheque deposit list; one cheque is marked bounced, the tenant
   and manager are notified and a follow-up task appears. *Wow 3.*
7. **Close:** an owner statement PDF for one owner.

## 6. Objections

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Excel works for us." | It works until someone is on leave. The demo shows the three jobs Excel cannot do: reminding you, notifying the tenant, and building the owner statement. Your Excel files are the migration input. |
| "We already use Zoho / QuickBooks." | Keep it for the books. We cover leases, cheques, tenants and maintenance, and give your accountant a VAT summary per entity; accounting export is on the roadmap. |
| "We use Yardi / a big ERP." | Then we are probably not for you. We fit managers who want something lighter and UAE-specific. |
| "Is our data safe? Where is it?" | Each client has its own deployment, hosted in the UAE, with backups at a second UAE provider. Staff log in with two-factor authentication; every action is logged. |
| "Does it register Ejari?" | You record the Ejari number and certificate on the lease, and the system flags leases without one. Direct Ejari integration is on the roadmap. |
| "Can tenants pay online?" | On the roadmap. Today the system handles cheques, transfers (matched from your bank statement) and cash. |
| "We also manage service charges for owners associations." | Not covered. We help with the leasing and rental side of your business. |
| "How long does it take to start?" | Your units, tenants, leases and cheques are imported from Excel; then the team is trained by role. |
| "What does it cost?" | Priced per managed unit, with plans by team size. We quote after the demo. |
