# Glossary

Terms used across the documents. Each vertical adds its own terms here as it is documented.
Entries marked `✔ [Sn]` were checked against the source listed under Sources at the end.
Entries marked `⚠ open: <owner>` cannot be settled by public research; the owner (lawyer,
accountant, authority, client or onboarding) answers them.

| Term | Meaning | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| **86** | Kitchen slang for marking a menu item as unavailable because it has run out | F&B |
| **ADAFSA** | Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority | F&B |
| **ACTVET** | Abu Dhabi Centre for Technical and Vocational Education and Training: licenses and accredits training centres and tutors in Abu Dhabi | edtech |
| **ADEK** | Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge: licenses private schools in Abu Dhabi (training centres are licensed by ACTVET) | edtech |
| **ADHICS** | Abu Dhabi Healthcare Information and Cyber Security Standard issued by DoH. Version 2.0 (effective August 2024) also covers third-party and SaaS systems used by Abu Dhabi facilities ✔ [S1] | clinics |
| **Advance payment guarantee** | Bank guarantee given to the client in exchange for an advance payment; reduced as the advance is recovered from IPAs | construction |
| **AED** | UAE dirham, the currency. Written `AED 1,250.00` | all |
| **Aftermarket part** | A part made by a third party to fit a vehicle, as an alternative to the vehicle maker's genuine part | auto |
| **Agency repair** | Repair at the vehicle brand's authorised dealer. Under the unified motor policy, a third-party claim on a car in its first year of registration is repaired at the agency; for own-damage claims it is an option of the policy ✔ [S2] | auto |
| **Aggregator** | A third-party delivery platform (Talabat, Careem Food, Deliveroo, Noon Food) that sells a restaurant's food for a commission | F&B |
| **AMC** | Annual maintenance contract with a vendor: scope, assets covered, planned visits, SLA and cost | property, clinics, F&B, manufacturing |
| **AWB** | Air waybill: the transport document for air cargo | logistics |
| **Batch** | One run of a course with a fixed group, timetable, tutor and room | edtech |
| **Bench** | Employed workers not currently deployed to a client | manpower |
| **BL** | Bill of lading: the transport document and title for sea cargo | logistics |
| **BOM** | Bill of materials: the components and quantities needed to make one unit of a product, versioned | manufacturing, F&B (recipes) |
| **BOQ** | Bill of quantities: the itemised list of work with units, quantities and rates that a contract is priced from | construction |
| **BSP** | Business Solution Provider: a company licensed by Meta to send WhatsApp Business messages (e.g. Twilio, 360dialog) | all |
| **Capability** | Something a business must be able to do, independent of who does it or with which software. Identified as `DOMAIN.AREA.NN` | all |
| **CDD** | Customer due diligence: identifying and verifying a client and its beneficial owners, and assessing its money-laundering risk | accounting |
| **Certification** | The main contractor's or consultant's confirmation of the amount payable for an IPA, often lower than claimed | construction |
| **Change of status** | Converting a person already in the UAE from a visit or other visa to an employment residence visa without leaving the country | manpower |
| **Cloud kitchen** | A delivery-only kitchen with no dining room, often cooking for several virtual brands | F&B |
| **COA** | Certificate of analysis (or conformity): test results for one lot, often required by B2B customers | manufacturing |
| **COD** | Cash on delivery: the courier collects payment from the recipient and remits it to the seller | logistics |
| **Consignee** | The party a shipment is delivered to | logistics |
| **Copay / deductible** | The part of an insured service's cost paid by the patient, as a fixed amount, a percentage, or an amount before cover starts | clinics |
| **Corporate tax** | UAE federal tax of 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 (0% up to that amount), for financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023 ✔ [S3] | all |
| **Covers** | Number of guests served in a dine-in service | F&B |
| **CPT** | Current Procedural Terminology: codes for medical services and procedures used on claims | clinics |
| **Credit hold** | A block on a customer's new orders because they exceed their credit limit or have overdue invoices | auto, manufacturing |
| **Cross-reference** | A link between equivalent part numbers from different brands or quality grades (also "interchange") | auto |
| **CSAT** | Customer satisfaction score from ratings after a completed service | clinics, F&B, auto, others |
| **Darb** | Abu Dhabi's road toll system | logistics |
| **DCD** | Dubai Civil Defence: approves fire and life-safety aspects of fit-out works | construction |
| **Dead stock** | Items with no sale or issue for a long period (e.g. 180 days) | auto, others |
| **Delivery order (DO)** | Document from the shipping line or agent releasing cargo to the consignee after clearance | logistics |
| **Deployment** | Placement of a worker at a client site for a role, shift and period | manpower |
| **DEWA** | Dubai Electricity and Water Authority | property |
| **DHA** | Dubai Health Authority: licenses health facilities and professionals in Dubai and runs eClaimLink and NABIDH | clinics |
| **DHPO** | Dubai Health Post Office: the exchange behind eClaimLink for claims, prior authorisations and remittances ✔ [S4] | clinics |
| **Disbursement** | A cost paid on a client's behalf and recharged at cost | logistics, accounting |
| **DLD** | Dubai Land Department, which runs Ejari and regulates real estate through RERA | property |
| **DLP** | Defects liability period: typically 12 months after practical completion during which the contractor fixes defects | construction |
| **DNFBP** | Designated non-financial business or profession (e.g. accountants, auditors, real estate brokers, dealers in precious metals), subject to AML/CFT obligations | accounting, property |
| **DoH** | Department of Health – Abu Dhabi: health regulator for Abu Dhabi; runs Shafafiya and Malaffi | clinics |
| **E-invoicing (FTA)** | The UAE mandate to issue B2B and B2G invoices electronically in a structured format through an Accredited Service Provider. Pilot from 1 July 2026; mandatory from 1 January 2027 (revenue ≥ AED 50m) and 1 July 2027 (others) | all |
| **e-POD** | Electronic proof of delivery: photo, signature or OTP, time and location | logistics, F&B, auto |
| **ECAS** | Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme: MoIAT's mandatory conformity certificate for regulated products, imported or made locally; valid 1 year ✔ [S5] [S6] | manufacturing |
| **eClaimLink** | Dubai's electronic health insurance transaction platform for claims and prior authorisations | clinics |
| **EDD** | Enhanced due diligence: extra checks and approvals for high-risk clients | accounting |
| **Ejari** | Dubai's system for registering tenancy contracts (DLD). Needed for DEWA, residence visas and school registration. Has an official API for management companies | property |
| **EmaraTax** | The FTA's online portal for tax registration, returns and payments | accounting, all |
| **Emirates ID** | National identity card for citizens and residents, number format `784-YYYY-NNNNNNN-C` | all |
| **EMR** | Electronic medical record: the licensed clinical system holding notes, diagnoses, prescriptions and results | clinics |
| **End-of-service gratuity** | Lump sum due to an employee at exit: 21 days' basic salary per year for the first five years, 30 days per year after that, capped at two years' wages ✔ [S7] | manpower, all |
| **Entitlement** | Platform term: what a client's plan allows (modules, users, entities, limits), enforced by the kernel and set by the operator | platform |
| **Entry permit** | Permit allowing a recruited worker to enter the UAE to complete the residence visa process | manpower |
| **EQM** | Emirates Quality Mark: MoIAT's higher-level mark for products made to UAE standards, granted after a factory audit; the licence is valid 3 years ✔ [S5] [S6] | manufacturing |
| **Excess (deductible)** | The part of an insured repair the policyholder pays; the insurer pays the rest | auto |
| **Fit-out** | Interior works that make a shell space usable: partitions, ceilings, MEP, joinery, finishes | construction |
| **Fitment** | The list of vehicles (make, model, year, engine) a part fits | auto |
| **Flat-rate labour** | Workshop labour charged at a fixed number of hours per operation, regardless of actual time | auto |
| **FoodWatch** | Dubai Municipality's digital platform for establishment registration, food handler training, PIC reporting and inspections ✔ [S8] | F&B |
| **Freight forwarder** | A company that arranges sea, air or road transport and customs clearance for shippers | logistics |
| **FTA** | Federal Tax Authority: VAT and corporate tax | all |
| **FTL / LTL** | Full truckload / less than truckload | logistics |
| **GDRFA** | General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (Dubai): residence visas | manpower |
| **Genuine / OEM part** | A part supplied by the vehicle maker (genuine) or by the maker's original supplier (OEM) | auto |
| **goAML** | The Financial Intelligence Unit's system for filing suspicious transaction reports | accounting |
| **GRN** | Goods received note: record of a delivery against a purchase order | construction, manufacturing, F&B, auto |
| **Guardian** | A parent or other adult who acts for a minor or dependant: enrols, books, pays and receives reports | edtech, clinics |
| **HIE** | Health information exchange: the regulator-run platform where facilities share clinical data (NABIDH, Malaffi, Riayati) | clinics |
| **ICD-10** | International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision: diagnosis codes used on claims | clinics |
| **ICP** | Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security: Emirates ID and residence outside Dubai | manpower |
| **ICV** | In-Country Value: a MoIAT programme certificate, issued by authorised certifying bodies from audited financial statements and valid 14 months from their date. It scores local spend, Emirati employment and local production, and is used in tenders ✔ [S9] | manufacturing |
| **ILOE** | Involuntary Loss of Employment scheme: unemployment insurance, mandatory since 1 January 2023 for private-sector and federal government employees (Federal Decree-Law 13 of 2022). Subscribing is the employee's own obligation ✔ [S10] | manpower |
| **Imprest** | Petty cash model where the float is topped up to a fixed amount by the value of approved spending | all |
| **IPA** | Interim payment application: a periodic (usually monthly) claim for work done to date | construction |
| **Job card** | The workshop work order for one vehicle visit: check-in, complaints, labour, parts, QC | auto |
| **KDS** | Kitchen display system: screens that show order tickets per station instead of printed tickets | F&B |
| **KHDA** | Knowledge and Human Development Authority: licenses private schools, training institutes and tutoring centres in Dubai | edtech |
| **KPI pack** | Periodic management report for a client: key figures, trends and commentary | accounting |
| **Landed cost** | Purchase price plus freight, duty and clearing charges, spread over the items received | auto, others |
| **Last mile** | The final delivery leg from a hub to the recipient | logistics |
| **Legal entity** | Platform term: one trade licence with its own TRN, numbering and bank accounts; a client may have several ([decision 0003](decisions/0003-legal-entities.md)) | all |
| **Lot** | A batch of one item with a number, status and expiry, used for traceability | manufacturing, F&B, clinics |
| **LPO** | Local purchase order: a purchase order, also used to award subcontract work | construction, others |
| **Makani** | Dubai's geographic addressing number for buildings | logistics, property |
| **Make to order** | Production started only against a confirmed customer order | manufacturing |
| **Make-up session** | A session attended in place of one missed, often in another batch | edtech |
| **Malaffi** | Abu Dhabi's health information exchange | clinics |
| **Medical fitness test** | Mandatory health screening for a residence visa | manpower |
| **Menu engineering** | Analysis of menu items by popularity and margin to decide prices and placement | F&B |
| **MEP** | Mechanical, electrical and plumbing works | construction |
| **Midday break** | Mandatory break from outdoor work in direct sun, 12:30 to 15:00 from 15 June to 15 September, announced yearly by MoHRE ✔ [S11] | construction, logistics |
| **Mirsal** | Dubai Customs' declaration system; the current version is Mirsal 2, used through the Dubai Trade portal ✔ [S12] | logistics |
| **MLRO** | Money laundering reporting officer: the compliance officer responsible for AML and for reporting suspicions | accounting |
| **MOHAP** | Ministry of Health and Prevention: licenses private health facilities in Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. Sharjah licenses its own through the Sharjah Health Authority since 2025 ✔ [S13] | clinics |
| **MoHRE** | Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation: labour law, work permits, WPS | construction, manpower |
| **MoIAT** | Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology: industrial licences, standards and conformity | manufacturing |
| **Mollak** | DLD (RERA) system for service charges in Dubai's jointly owned properties: approves service charge budgets, sends quarterly invoices to owners and holds management companies' statements and contracts ✔ [S14] | property |
| **MRN** | Medical record number: the clinic's identifier for a patient | clinics |
| **MT940** | SWIFT bank statement file format, one of the formats for statement import. Some UAE banks offer it (e.g. Commercial Bank of Dubai) ✔ [S15]; support per bank is checked during onboarding `⚠ open: onboarding (O-02)` | all |
| **Mulkiya** | Vehicle registration card issued by the traffic authority | logistics, auto |
| **MVP** | Minimum viable product: the first version that can be sold | all |
| **NABIDH** | Dubai's health information exchange | clinics |
| **Nafis** | Federal programme supporting Emiratisation in the private sector. Companies with 50 or more employees add 2% Emiratis in skilled roles each year (10% by the end of 2026) ✔ [S16] | manpower |
| **NCR** | Non-conformance report: a recorded quality failure and its disposition (rework, scrap, use as is, return) | manufacturing |
| **NOC** | No-objection certificate from a landlord, developer or authority | construction, others |
| **Operation 300bn** | The UAE national industrial strategy, run by MoIAT, to grow industry's contribution to GDP from AED 133bn (2021) to AED 300bn by 2031 ✔ [S17] | manufacturing |
| **Operator** | Platform term: us, the company that hosts and sells the platform, using `wl-operator` | platform |
| **Order middleware** | A service (e.g. Grubtech, Deliverect, UrbanPiper) that connects aggregator orders and menus to a restaurant's systems | F&B |
| **Par level** | The stock quantity an outlet should hold for an item; reordering tops it back up | F&B |
| **PBC list** | "Prepared by client" list: the documents a client must provide for a period | accounting |
| **PDC** | Post-dated cheque: a cheque dated in the future, common for rent and B2B payments in the UAE | property, others |
| **PDPL** | UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law 45 of 2021) | all |
| **Performance bond** | Bank guarantee (often 10% of the contract) securing the contractor's performance; released after completion | construction |
| **PIC** | Person in charge: a Dubai Municipality–certified food safety staff member required on site on each shift ✔ [S18] | F&B |
| **Placement test** | A test that sets a new student's level before enrolment | edtech |
| **PPM** | Planned preventive maintenance: maintenance done on a schedule rather than on request | property, manufacturing, auto |
| **Practical completion** | The point at which the works are handed over for use, possibly with minor snags; starts the DLP | construction |
| **Prior authorisation** | A payer's approval obtained before a service is provided, required for some services to be covered | clinics |
| **Private tutoring permit** | Free MoHRE permit (with the Ministry of Education) for individuals tutoring outside a licensed centre; valid two years, good-conduct certificate required ✔ [S19] | edtech |
| **QFZP** | Qualifying free zone person: a free-zone entity that meets the conditions (qualifying activities, substance, non-qualifying revenue within 5% or AED 5m) and pays 0% corporate tax on qualifying income ✔ [S20] | accounting |
| **Rate card** | A contract's prices per service, role or shift, with multipliers and minimums, used to price usage | manpower, logistics |
| **RDC** | Rental Dispute Centre (Dubai) | property |
| **Re-export** | Goods imported into the UAE and sold onward to another country, common in the Deira and Sharjah parts trade | auto |
| **Realisation** | Billed value as a share of the standard value of time worked | accounting |
| **Remittance advice** | The payer's statement of what was paid and denied on submitted claims, with reasons | clinics |
| **Rent roll** | List of all units with tenant, rent and lease dates | property |
| **RERA** | Real Estate Regulatory Agency, part of DLD: licenses property managers and publishes the rental index | property |
| **Retention** | A percentage (often 5–10%) withheld from each payment, released at practical completion and at the end of the DLP | construction |
| **RFI** | Request for information sent to the consultant about drawings or specifications | construction |
| **Riayati** | MOHAP's unified health record platform for MOHAP and EHS facilities, which also hosts its e-Claims Post Office ✔ [S21] | clinics |
| **Run** | A driver's trip with an ordered list of stops; printed as a run sheet | logistics |
| **Salik** | Dubai's road toll system | logistics, auto |
| **Sanctions screening** | Checking names against sanctions and terrorist lists (UN consolidated list, UAE Local Terrorist List) | accounting, all |
| **Session package** | A prepaid number of sessions used within a validity period | edtech, clinics |
| **Shafafiya** | Abu Dhabi's health insurance transaction platform (DoH) | clinics |
| **SIF** | Salary information file: the file format submitted to the bank or exchange house for WPS | construction, manpower |
| **SIRA** | Security Industry Regulatory Agency (Dubai): licenses security companies and guards | manpower |
| **Snag** | A defect or incomplete item found at inspection before handover | construction, property |
| **SPEA** | Sharjah Private Education Authority: licenses private schools and training centres in Sharjah, including its free zones | edtech |
| **STR** | Suspicious transaction report, filed through goAML | accounting |
| **Subject** | Platform term: the record a document, ticket or invoice is attached to (e.g. a lease or an employee), stored as `subject_type` + `subject_id` | all |
| **Submittal** | Shop drawings, samples or material data submitted to the consultant for approval | construction |
| **Supplementary** | An extra estimate for damage found after the insurer's original approval | auto |
| **Surveyor** | The insurer's assessor who inspects the damage and approves the estimate | auto |
| **Tadbeer** | Government-licensed centres for domestic worker recruitment (out of scope) | manpower |
| **Tasheel / Amer** | Typing and service centres for MoHRE and immigration transactions | manpower |
| **Tawtheeq** | Abu Dhabi's tenancy contract registration, done by the landlord (or its manager) on DARI or TAMM; needed for ADDC utilities | property |
| **Tax agent** | A person registered with the FTA to represent taxpayers before it | accounting |
| **Tax period** | The period a VAT return covers (usually quarterly), staggered per taxpayer | accounting, all |
| **TecDoc** | A commercial database of aftermarket parts, fitment and cross-references, licensed from TecAlliance; price depends on data volume, number of users and distribution, and is negotiated ✔ [S22] | auto |
| **Telematics** | GPS devices fitted to vehicles that report location, speed and odometer | logistics, auto |
| **Theoretical food cost** | The cost of ingredients that should have been used, calculated from sales × recipes; compared with actual usage | F&B |
| **Three-way match** | Checking a supplier bill against the purchase order and the goods received before paying it | construction, manufacturing, F&B, auto |
| **Total loss** | The insurer's decision that a vehicle is not worth repairing | auto |
| **TPA** | Third-party administrator: processes claims on behalf of an insurer | clinics |
| **Trial balance** | List of all ledger account balances at a date, used to build KPI packs | accounting |
| **TRN** | Tax Registration Number, 15 digits, issued by the FTA to VAT-registered businesses | all |
| **UBO** | Ultimate beneficial owner: the natural person who ultimately owns or controls a company | accounting, all |
| **VAT** | Value-added tax, 5% standard rate. Supplies can be standard-rated, zero-rated or exempt | all |
| **Vertical** | One of the ten target business types (property, construction, …) | all |
| **VIN** | Vehicle identification number: the 17-character code that identifies a vehicle | auto |
| **Virtual brand** | A delivery-only brand cooked in an existing kitchen, with its own menu and listing | F&B |
| **Visa case** | Platform term: one visa process (new, renewal, cancellation) tracked as steps with documents, dates and fees | manpower |
| **VO** | Variation order: an approved change to the scope or price of a contract | construction |
| **Voluntary disclosure** | Correction of an error in a filed tax return, submitted to the FTA | accounting |
| **Waybill** | Document that travels with a shipment and identifies it by barcode | logistics |
| **WIP** | Work in progress: logged time not yet billed | accounting |
| **Work order** | Instruction to produce a quantity of an item on a line from a BOM version | manufacturing |
| **Work permit** | MoHRE authorisation for a company to employ a specific foreign worker | manpower |
| **WPS** | Wage Protection System: salaries paid through approved banks or exchange houses so MoHRE can verify payment | construction, manpower |
| **Z report** | End-of-day sales and payment summary for an outlet | F&B |
