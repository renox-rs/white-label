# property

The vertical app for property management companies ([spec](../../docs/verticals/01-property.md)):
the portfolio, the lease and its lifecycle, and the lease-to-cash cycle on top of the shared `wl-*`
modules. Property reaches the other modules only through the contracts of `wl-kernel` (the
invoicer, the cheque register, the deposit book, the document guard, the party directory, tasks and
notifications), never through their crates.

The host mounts it when `client.toml` names `app = "property"`:

```
use renox::prelude::*;

# let _ =
App::new().module(property::Property)
# ;
```

| `transfer_unit` (`PRP.PRT.03`): a unit leaves an `active` or `ending` agreement because it is sold or no longer managed; a property manager asks with the unit, the transfer date and the new owner (or "no longer managed"), another property manager approves, and nothing changes before. A sale the company still manages needs the new owner's agreement `active` with the unit listed from the transfer date (a row that waits, `awaiting_transfer`), else the request and the approval are refused with "new owner's agreement not active"; at the approval the unit goes out of the old agreement the day before and into the new one on the date, the scheduled charges from the date are recreated with the new owner's legal entity as issuer (issued invoices stay with the old owner), the cheques are relinked to them, the deposit stays held against the new owner's receipt and the tenants are told the new payee. "No longer managed" is the handover of `end` for that unit: a pack with a receipt per deposit and cheque, the lease marked "handed over", the cheques returned, the deposit transferred. When the last unit leaves, the agreement stays and the property managers get a task to give notice | §4.4, §7.1 *Management agreement* | `unit_transfer` |
