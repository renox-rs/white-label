# wl-inventory

Inventory for the white-label platform ([`docs/modules/wl-inventory.md`](../../docs/modules/wl-inventory.md)):
items, locations, purchase orders, goods receipts and the stock they put in
place. Quantities are kept in thousandths of the item's base unit, money in fils.

```
# use renox::prelude::*;
# let _ =
App::new().module(wl_inventory::WlInventory::new())
# ;
```

What is here so far:

| Part | Spec | Module |
|---|---|---|
| Permissions (`items.*`, `stock.view`, `purchase_orders.*`, `goods_receipts.*`, `supplier_returns.manage`) | §6 | `permissions` |
| The base entities receiving needs: `Item` (base unit, lot-tracked flag, moving weighted average cost), `Location` (type, scope, negative stock policy), `PurchaseOrder` with its lines and the machine's states, `Lot` and its machine's states, `StockMovement` (immutable; the balance of an item at a location is derived from the movements) | §3, §4 | `item`, `location`, `purchase_order`, `lot`, `movement` |
| Settings: the over-receipt tolerance (5%) and the rejection reasons, with the ones that need a reading or a photo | §7 | `config` |
| Posting a goods receipt: the storekeeper enters received, accepted and rejected quantity per line with a reason and what became of the rejected goods (*refused at the door* or *kept for collection*), and `post` (`goods_receipts.post` in the location's scope) puts only the accepted quantity into stock: a `receive` movement and a lot per accepted line, the item's average cost, the purchase order's `receive` (rejected quantity stays outstanding), and a draft Return to supplier for the door-refused lines and one for the lines kept for collection; `GoodsReceived` carries accepted and rejected quantity | §4 *Goods receipt*, §5.1, §5.6 | `goods_receipt`, `receipt_post`, `supplier_return`, `events` |
| Over-receipt (`STK.ACL.04`): an accepted quantity above the ordered one by more than the tolerance turns `post` into an approval request (`PLT.APR.01`) for a holder of `purchase_orders.issue` in the location's scope, never the poster; the receipt stays `draft` until `post_approved` | §4 *Goods receipt* | `receipt_post` |
| Reversing a posted goods receipt (`goods_receipts.reverse` in the location's scope): all or nothing, refused when a Return to supplier made from it is `dispatched` or `credited`, when its purchase order is `closed` (billed), or when part of the accepted quantity (of a line or lot) is no longer at the location or the lot is on hold, naming the line and the missing quantity; otherwise `adjust` movements take the accepted quantity out, the order's outstanding quantity goes back up, and the draft returns from the receipt are cancelled | §4 *Goods receipt* | `receipt_reverse` |
| Dispatching a Return to supplier (`supplier_returns.manage`): with a collection proof (the driver's signature, a courier number or the goods receipt), the lines taken from stock leave with `return_out` movements at the source receipt's cost (the difference to the average cost is kept on the line as the price variance), a `replace` line from stock gives its quantity back to its order line, a held lot with nothing left is `rejected`, and a return note PDF is kept; above AED 5,000 (`return_approval_fils`, `STK.ACL.06`) `dispatch` becomes an approval request for a second holder of the permission, never the requester. The goods refused at the door are dispatched by the system as the receipt posts (no movement, no approval); `SupplierReturnDispatched` carries the lines for the debit note. A return of stock is made with `supplier_return::create_from_stock` | §4 *Return to supplier*, §5.6 | `return_dispatch`, `return_note`, `supplier_return`, `events` |
| Cancelling and crediting a Return to supplier (`supplier_returns.manage`): `cancel` of a `draft` return needs a reason, releases the stock it held aside (`supplier_return::reserved_milli`) and, for goods rejected at receipt and kept for collection, asks whether they are received again (a draft goods receipt on the order) or discarded; the system withdraws the draft returns of a reversed receipt. `credit` ends a `dispatched` return: the system's `credit_covered` when the supplier's debit notes (`SupplierDebitNoteIssued`, which now names the debited lines) cover every `credit` line and replacement receipts cover every `replace` line, coverage being kept per line; or by hand with a reason (*refund received* or *supplier refused*), a refused credit posting the uncovered value as wastage through the `CostPoster` and, above AED 1,000 (`wastage_approval_fils`, `STK.ACL.02`), asking a second holder of the permission first. `SupplierDebitNoteCancelled` leaves the return `dispatched` (its cover is taken off) and gives procurement a task; `SupplierReturnCredited` follows each credit | §4 *Return to supplier*, §6 | `return_credit`, `supplier_return`, `events` |
| A free replacement against a dispatched Return to supplier (`goods_receipt::create_replacement_draft`, then the same `post`): no purchase order, so nothing is received on an order or left to match in wl-invoicing; the lines are `replace` lines of the return that have no order line, or whose order is `closed`, and what is received of a line never goes above what is still to be replaced. The accepted quantity comes in at zero cost (the item's average cost is diluted), is counted on the return line, goods rejected again go on a new return, and the return is `credited` when every line is covered; such a receipt cannot be reversed | §4 *Goods receipt* `post`, §5.6 | `goods_receipt`, `receipt_post`, `events` |
| `STK.RUL.08`, chasing a return that stays `dispatched` (`chase`): daily at 07:00 Asia/Dubai, a return `dispatched` for more than N days (`return_chase_days`, default 30, calendar days from the dispatch date) notifies procurement and finance and gives procurement one task to chase the supplier (one task per return, resolved when it is credited); the notice repeats every N days from the last one (days N + 1, 2N + 1...) until the return leaves `dispatched` | §6 | `chase`, `config` |
| Reopening a received purchase order: the system's `reopen` (`received` to `partially_received`) runs when a `replace` return from stock is dispatched or a receipt is reversed; the returned quantity is outstanding again and `committed_value` (outstanding at the order's prices) is back; a line restores once, a `closed` order is never reopened | §4 *Purchase order* | `purchase_order` |
| Revising an issued purchase order (`po_revise`, `purchase_orders.issue`): `revise` on an `issued` or `partially_received` order with a reason, new quantities and prices per line and, optionally, a later expected date that records who asked for it (*us* or *the supplier*); refused when the currency changes, a line goes below what it accepted, or nothing changes. The earlier revision is kept read-only (`PurchaseOrderRevision`), the revision number goes up, the PDF is marked "Revision N" and the supplier is told with its link, committed cost follows the lines, and `PurchaseOrderRevised` carries the changed lines and the old and new total. A higher total or a later date passes the block guard (`PTY.ACL.03`); a higher total above a limit (`order_limits_fils`, default AED 10,000 and 50,000, `STK.ACL.03`) becomes an approval request with a step per limit the new total is above, never approved by the requester, and the revision in force stays until the last approval, when the quantities are checked again against what was accepted meanwhile | §4 *Purchase order*, §5.1 | `po_revise`, `purchase_order`, `events`, `pages` |
| Landed costs (`landed_cost`, `landed_adjust`, `landed_costs.post`): a draft with its receipts and cost lines (freight, duty, clearing, insurance) is `allocate`d on a basis (`value`, `quantity`, or `weight` / `volume` given per receipt line): the total is spread over the accepted receipt lines (each share rounded down, the few fils left to the largest share, the first on a tie), and per item and location the share on the quantity still on hand (the smaller of what is on hand at the receipt's location now and what the receipts brought, over what they brought) re-values the item's average cost while the share on the quantity already consumed is the landed cost variance, or goes to the cost object charged on receipt (the scope of a site store's receipt), through the `CostPoster` after the commit; `LandedCostAllocated` follows. A late or corrected cost is a `LandedCostAdjustment` of cost lines (negative for a refund) with the supplier bill's reference and a posting date: `adjust` refuses a date in a filed VAT period (the kernel's `PeriodLocks`, answered by wl-invoicing), spreads the total on the original basis, splits it the same way, never changes earlier movements or the allocation, and a refund re-values stock down to zero value at most. Above AED 5,000 (`landed_cost_approval_fils`, `STK.ACL.06`, the absolute amount) `adjust` becomes an approval request for a second holder of `landed_costs.post`, never the requester; a rejection leaves the draft. `LandedCostAdjusted` shows what re-valued stock and what went to the variance. The module tells wl-invoicing which landed costs exist (`LandedCosts`) | §3, §4 *Landed cost*, §5.8 | `landed_cost`, `landed_adjust`, `events`, `pages` |
| Drafting an adjustment from a late supplier bill (`landed_draft`): when wl-invoicing approves a bill (`SupplierBillApproved`) with lines coded to a landed cost, an `allocated` landed cost gets one **draft** adjustment per bill (the same event again does nothing; another bill makes its own draft, never merged) with a cost line per coded bill line (amount and invoice number read from the bill line through `SupplierBills`, the kind told from its description) and the holders of the `procurement` role are told; a landed cost still in `draft` takes the bill line as a cost line instead. Nothing is posted by itself: the purchaser posts the draft and `STK.ACL.06` applies then | §4 *Landed cost*, §5.8 | `landed_draft`, `events` |
| Issuing a purchase order to a supplier that may be blocked (`po_issue`, `purchase_orders.issue`): `issue` takes a `draft` order with a supplier, a delivery location and priced lines to `issued`, numbers it from the `PO-` series and keeps the PDF of revision 1. It calls the block guard first (`BlockGuard::check(supplier, start)`, `PTY.ACL.03`), before any other check: a blocked supplier is refused with the neutral *on hold* text (the real reasons only to a holder of `parties.block_reason.view`) and the offer to request an exception; the same form posted with `block_exception=1` becomes an approval request of `parties.block_exception` for the role the guard names, and on approval the system's `issue_approved` issues the order once. A goods receipt against an issued order and a return to the supplier are never refused; a revision that raises the total or moves the date later is refused for a blocked supplier (see `po_revise`) and a lower total applies | §4 *Purchase order*, wl-parties §6 | `po_issue`, `po_revise`, `pages` |
| Suppliers per item and reorder suggestions (`item_supplier`, `reorder`, `supplier_pages`, `item_suppliers.manage`): one `preferred` and ranked `alternative` suppliers per item with the supplier's item code, purchase unit, lead time, minimum order quantity and the document kinds it should hold; one row per item and supplier, the first preferred supplier set directly, any later change or the deactivation of the preferred row a `PreferredSupplierRequest` for a second person (BL-STK-31) while the row stays in use; alternatives renumbered 1..n on save, a deactivated row keeping its rank; all audited. The last price is derived from the latest posted receipt line, else the latest issued order line, and shown only to a holder of `stock.cost.view`. `STK.RUL.04` (daily 07:00 Asia/Dubai, `reorder::TASK`) writes one open `ReorderSuggestion` per item and location at or below the item's reorder point (up to its maximum or par, raised to the supplier's minimum order with the rounding shown): it goes to the preferred supplier, or to the first active alternative that passes `BlockGuard::check(supplier, start)` marked *alternative* with the reason *preferred supplier on hold* (or *inactive*), or has no supplier; a blocked supplier is listed as *on hold* only; the policy *reorder point + lead time* takes the lead time of the supplier chosen; an expired document kind is a warning that blocks nothing; the purchaser can switch a suggestion to another listed supplier with a reason (audited, nothing changes on the item, counted for the scorecard per purchaser and month). `PartiesMerged` moves the rows of the merged supplier to the survivor, the stronger role staying | §3, §5.9 steps 1 and 3, §6 | `item_supplier`, `reorder`, `supplier_pages`, `item` |
| The supplier score and its monthly snapshots (`supplier_score`, `supplier_scores.view`): per supplier, over the last three calendar months, the share of receipts on time (the day the goods arrived, `arrived_at`, against the expected date of the first issued revision plus one grace day; a later date counts only when a revision records *requested by us*), the rejected value (rejections at receipt except `excess`, plus the lines of a dispatched Return to supplier taken from stock, over the value received at the order's price), the quality incidents reported through the `SupplierIncidentSource` extension point (the kernel's, provided by a vertical; voided ones drop out), the debit notes (count and value, shown not scored, kept from `SupplierDebitNoteIssued` and `...Cancelled`) and the price variance of the order lines issued in the window against the last price before their issue (same currency only; only an increase lowers the score). Score = 40 on time + 30 rejected + 15 incidents + 15 price (caps 10%, 3, 10%), a component without data dropping out with its weight spread, rounded half up, none under 3 receipts (`not_enough_data`); bands `good` 85+, `watch`, `below_threshold` under 70 (`ScoreSettings`, `WlInventory::supplier_score`). A daily run at 06:00 Asia/Dubai (`supplier_score::TASK`) builds the missing snapshots, once per supplier and month, from the month after the supplier's first receipt, so a missed day 1 is caught up; a snapshot is never recomputed, a review note is added beside it. Read with `GET /suppliers/{party}/score`: the values need `stock.cost.view` | §3, §5.9 step 2, §8 | `supplier_score`, `supplier_pages`, `goods_receipt`, `item_supplier` |
| The supplier bill match reads the ordered and accepted quantity of an order's lines through the kernel's `PurchaseMatchSource`; rejected goods are not in it | §6 | `match_source` |

Not built yet: the value limits and segregation of duties of `issue`, `short_close`, `close` and `cancel` of a purchase order, the screens of the field PWA and the other entities.

The settings are client defaults set in code (§7); the host changes one with a builder call:

```
# let _ =
wl_inventory::WlInventory::new()
    .over_receipt_tolerance_bps(1_000)
    .rejection_reason("temperature", true)
# ;
```

The module owns `src/texts.rs`, the English texts (`wl_inventory.*`). The app changes or
translates them in `resources/lang/{locale}.json`.
