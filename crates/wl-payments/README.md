# wl-payments

Payments: money received from or paid to a party by cash, transfer, cheque or card, receipts
numbered per legal entity, allocation to invoices, reversal, refunds and the party's credit
([spec](../../docs/modules/wl-payments.md)). Other modules record payments and read credit
only through the kernel (`wl-kernel`), never through this crate.

The host mounts it when `client.toml` enables `payments`:

```
use renox::prelude::*;

# let _ =
App::new().module(wl_payments::WlPayments::new())
# ;
```

What is here so far:

| `FIN.RUL.18`: a bounced cheque is chased, hourly: 3 days after the bounce (Asia/Dubai) the party is reminded with the open amount and the bounce fee (not while blocked) and the responsible staff are told; 14 days after it the accountant and the manager each get a task to re-present, refer to legal or write off (`WlPayments::cheque_chase`); only while the cheque is `bounced`, counted again from a new bounce | §6, §7 | `cheque_chase` |

| Owner drawings (`FIN.ACL.21`, `FIN.ACL.23`): an outgoing payment with purpose *owner drawings* is saved as a `PaymentDrawing` (the details entered; no payment) and the approval is asked of wl-invoicing through the kernel's `OwnerDrawings`; only the final approval records it (`record` has the guard `drawing_approved`, a sole owner's own drawing is recorded at once and flagged), a rejection, withdrawal or expiry records nothing; the payee is the drawings category's owner party, the amount in AED, no allocation; a repayment by the owner is an incoming payment with the same purpose and needs no approval; the issued cheque of an approved drawing is registered to the owner party for the approved amount (`RegisterCheque::drawing`) and its `clear` records the drawing; reversing a drawing tells the holders of `drawings.approve`; the drawings payments are read by budget vs actual through the kernel's `DrawingPaymentSource`; the seeded expense categories include *Owner drawings* (VAT blocked, no cost object).
